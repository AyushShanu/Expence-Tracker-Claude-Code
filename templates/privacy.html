Privacy and
Data Protection

Learn how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal and financial information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and financial transaction data you choose to track. We also automatically collect certain usage data like IP address, browser type, and interaction patterns to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to provide, maintain, and improve the Spendly service, to communicate with you about updates and security notices, and to analyze usage trends. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, conducting analytics, or providing customer support, provided they agree to keep your data confidential. We will not share your data with any other third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. This includes encryption in transit and at rest, regular security audits, and strict access controls.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also opt out of certain data processing activities. To exercise these activities, please contact us using the information below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
